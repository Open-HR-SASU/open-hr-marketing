## 2. What Is a Cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. Cookies allow the site to recognise your device and remember certain information about your visit.

Cookies may be:
- **Session cookies:** automatically deleted when you close your browser
- **Persistent cookies:** remain on your device until they expire or are manually deleted

## 3. Our Approach: Privacy by Design

Open HR adopts a minimalist approach to cookies, prioritising respect for your privacy. We use alternative technologies that do not require intrusive tracking cookies.

### 3.1 No Advertising Cookies

We use **no advertising cookies** and no trackers for targeted advertising or commercial profiling purposes.

### 3.2 Cookie-Free Analytics

To measure our Site's audience, we use **Plausible Analytics**, a privacy-respecting web analytics solution that:
- Places **no cookies** on your device
- Collects **no personal data**
- Is GDPR and UK GDPR compliant without requiring consent
- Is hosted within the European Union

This approach allows us to understand how our Site is used whilst fully respecting your privacy.

## 4. Cookies Used on the Site

### 4.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies (Essential)

These cookies are essential for the Site to function. They cannot be disabled as they enable the use of essential features.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|-------------|---------|----------|------|
| `locale` | Remembers your language preference | Session | First-party |
| `session_token` | Secure session identification (application platform) | Session | First-party |
| `csrf_token` | Protection against CSRF attacks | Session | First-party |

### 4.2 Authentication Cookies

When you log into your Open HR account on the application platform, the following cookies are used:

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|-------------|---------|----------|------|
| `auth_session` | Maintains your authenticated session | 7 days (or until logout) | First-party, HttpOnly, Secure |
| `refresh_token` | Secure session renewal | 30 days | First-party, HttpOnly, Secure |

**Important note:** Open HR uses an internally developed authentication system. No third-party authentication service cookies are placed.

### 4.3 Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your choices and preferences to improve your experience.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|-------------|---------|----------|------|
| `theme` | Remembers your theme preference (light/dark) | 1 year | First-party |
| `cookie_consent` | Remembers your cookie choices | 1 year | First-party |

## 7. Managing Your Preferences

### 7.1 Browser Settings

You can configure your browser to accept or reject cookies. Here is how to do so for the most common browsers:

- **Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox:** Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
- **Edge:** Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies

### 7.2 Consequences of Refusing Cookies

Refusing strictly necessary cookies may prevent access to certain Site features, including:
- Logging into your account
- Remembering your preferred language
- Maintaining your session
