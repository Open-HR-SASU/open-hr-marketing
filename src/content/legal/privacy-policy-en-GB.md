## 5. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

### 5.1 The ProScore System

**In plain terms:** We use an algorithm to analyse responses from your former colleagues and calculate a professional reliability score.

Open HR uses automated processing to generate **ProScore** scores from reference questionnaire responses.

**How it works:**
- Several people who have supervised you respond to a questionnaire
- Their responses are analysed by our algorithm according to scientifically validated criteria
- A score from 0 to 100 is calculated to reflect your professional reliability
- Questions are adapted to your sector and the position applied for

**Important points:**
- The ProScore is a **decision-support tool**, not the sole determining factor
- Employers make final hiring decisions; the ProScore is one element among others
- The score has no direct legal effect on your situation

### 5.2 Your Rights Regarding Automated Decisions

In accordance with Article 22 of the UK GDPR, you have the right to:
- **Obtain human intervention** to review any assessment
- **Express your point of view** regarding the score assigned
- **Contest any decision** based on automated processing

To exercise these rights, contact: dpo@open-hr.work

## 7. International Data Transfers

### 7.1 Principle

Open HR is committed to hosting your data within the European Union, in accordance with our data sovereignty commitment.

### 7.2 Regulated Exceptions

Certain service providers (notably Stripe) may process data in the United States. These transfers are governed by:
- **Standard Contractual Clauses** approved by the European Commission and recognised under UK law
- **Supplementary measures** for data protection
- **EU-US Data Privacy Framework** (where applicable)

### 7.3 UK-Specific Considerations

Following Brexit, the UK operates under the UK GDPR. The European Commission has granted the UK an adequacy decision, meaning data can flow freely between the EU and UK. We ensure all transfers comply with both UK and EU data protection requirements.
